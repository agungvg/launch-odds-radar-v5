How a launch is priced

What this page never says: that a token will launch. The odds are the peers' record (442 of 758 protocols record a token); the value is what the token would be worth if it launched, at the peers' multiple. Both are fitted on this snapshot alone (DefiLlama, 2026-11-14) and both are tested held-out above. The farm plans are costed, not promised: fees and hours are the plan's own figures, and the exit rule is printed on every row's detail view.
